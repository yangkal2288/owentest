# "Meet Vallamo": voiceover script

*For the external VO recording. The film is timed to this script. If a read runs longer or shorter, the edit gets re-timed to the real recording, so read it naturally rather than to a stopwatch.*

- **Master:** about 45 s
- **Words:** about 85 (a calm pace, leaving the music room to breathe)

---

## 1. Delivery notes

- **Voice:** Owen, or a British voice in the same register: warm, confident, unhurried. It should feel like explaining something you're proud of to a clinic owner, not "announcing".
- **Pace:** about 140–150 words a minute. Leave the marked pauses. The picture fills them.
- **Tone arc:**
  - lines 1–2: a knowing, slightly wry half-smile ("you know the feeling")
  - lines 3–6: bright and clear
  - line 7: settled, reassuring
  - line 8: quietly proud
  - line 9: friendly, not salesy
- **Emphasis** is marked in **bold**. Don't over-hit it.
- **Pronunciation:** "Vallamo" is **va-lah-mo** (three syllables: *va · lah · mo*), said as **one fluid word with no syllable stressed**, even and flowing, in a natural British accent. Record one clean take of just the word, 3 times, so the edit can choose the best.
- **Claims:** read the lines exactly as written. They're checked against what the product does today.

## 4. Recording spec (send these files back)

- **Format:** WAV, 48 kHz, 24-bit, mono. Peaks around −6 dBFS, **no processing** (no compression, EQ or noise reduction). The edit will clean and level it to −14 LUFS.
- **Room:** quiet and soft (a wardrobe full of clothes works). Mic about 15–20 cm away, slightly off-axis. Same mic and position for every take.
- **Record:**
  1. The **whole master script in one continuous take, 3 times:** calm, warm and bright.
  2. **Each numbered line on its own, 3 takes each,** with a 2-second gap between takes.
  3. The **alternatives** (section 3) and the **optional voice line**.
  4. **"Vallamo"** on its own, 3 takes (va-lah-mo).
  5. **10 seconds of room tone** (silence in the same room).
- **File names:**
  - `meet-vo_master_take1-calm.wav`
  - `meet-vo_line04_take2.wav`
  - `meet-vo_alt-hookB_take1.wav`
  - `meet-vo_roomtone.wav`
- Put everything in one folder and send it back. The edit picks the best reads, cuts only in natural pauses, and re-times picture and captions to the voice.
